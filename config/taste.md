## Rules of thumb

- Headliner unknown but support act is a yes -> maybe, say which act.
- Unknown act at a loved venue -> maybe only if the scene fits; otherwise no.
- Arena shows (Moda Center, Climate Pledge) -> only if the artist is in the yes list.
- Sold-out tag in the ticket URL is not a reason to hide a show; note it instead.
- Same artist, several metros: one decision in Portland closes the others (SKIP) or
  all of them (NEVER).
- Seattle / Eugene / Bend shows are only worth surfacing for yes-list artists or
  strong scene fits; "maybe" in a travel metro should be rare.
